10. Customer budget, solar price per kWp & commercial BESS BESS

Enter the customer's maximum investment. The app compares a full monthly-unit solar target against budget- and roof-limited alternatives. All prices are editable indicative turnkey installed prices, including assumed taxes; confirm GST treatment and vendor quotations before issuing a proposal.

Reference price guide (illustrative 2026 India C&I): on-grid solar ₹38k–48k/kWp for larger rooftops; installed BESS commonly ₹25k–40k+/kWh depending on scale and scope. Automatic small-cabinet rates are conservative assumptions. No GST should be added again if your input prices already include it. Solar power is rated kWp; BESS storage is rated kWh, while BESS PCS power is rated kW. The extra PCS rate defaults to zero to avoid double counting. Obtain a written supplier scope before quotation. No subsidy, loan interest, O&M, degradation or inflation is included in simple payback estimates.

Hybrid + commercial BESS package specifically designed for the chosen budget

This package jointly selects PV panels and commercial LFP BESS capacity within the customer budget, measured rooftop and editable installed prices. The full-target capacity and unserved electricity are shown separately. A BESS package should include battery racks, BMS, EMS, PCS, fire protection, cooling and commissioning, or any exclusions must be priced separately. Inverter power (kW) and storage duration (kWh) must be engineered. If grid power is unavailable, this hybrid package is comparison-only; assess off-grid engineering instead.

Monthly generation uses entered average yield × 30 days. Estimated self-use uses an early day/night energy balance (not hourly dispatch); export credit is included only when marked verified. Savings and payback exclude demand charges, taxes, equipment replacements, financing, and recurring O&M. Off-grid output must be validated through hourly/weather simulation, worst-day energy and starting-surge engineering. A small feasible PV size is not proof of full backup coverage.
